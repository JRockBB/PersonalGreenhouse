Monitoring, controls & procurement

Local PLC for plant-survival and safety functions; supervisory mini PC for logging, dashboard, forecasts, and computer vision.

Hardwired safety chain

  • Emergency stop, smoke, low reservoir level, pump overload
  • No-return-flow shutdown after 10–15 seconds
  • Leak and high-level lockout of top-off and dosing
  • Independent freeze thermostat and high-temperature vent override

Procurement sequence

  • Phase 1: PLC, survival sensors, leaks, pump + spare
  • Phase 2: climate actuators, PAR, lighting and power meters
  • Phase 3: pH/EC, top-off, nutrient A/B dosing
  • Phase 4: mini PC, PoE network and fixed cameras
